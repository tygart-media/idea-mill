# Assessment vs Study — HOA Reserve Checker

> Photograph an HOA budget or assessment coupon; get back whether the reserve contribution matches the reserve study, plus the lender's reserve test.

## The document

The annual HOA/condo budget, the reserve schedule, the assessment coupon, or the reserve study summary the board mailed. Fields extracted: assessment income, reserve contribution (own line), component list (if printed), and any waiver language. No login for the first answer. The essay advises starting with Florida condominiums.

## The public file

Several, applied by state tag:

1. **Florida SIRS + §718.112, Florida Statutes** — structural integrity reserve studies required; reserves mandatory for roof, painting, pavement regardless of cost, and any other item over $25,000 (raised from $10,000 by CS/CS/HB 913, Ch. 2025-175, effective July 1, 2025). For budgets adopted on/after Dec 31, 2024, members may NOT vote to waive/reduce SIRS-item reserves. First SIRS deadline: Dec 31, 2025 (extended from 2024).
2. **Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-03** (March 18, 2026) — Full Review reserve test rises from 10% to 15% of budgeted assessment income for applications on/after January 4, 2027 (Freddie Mac aligned). A reserve study <3 years old funded at the study's highest recommended level can substitute. Limited Review retired for established condos for applications on/after August 3, 2026.
3. **California Civil Code 5551** — balcony/deck/elevated-walkway inspections required by January 1, 2025 (month-3 extension).
4. Reference distributions: Community Association Fact Book 2025 (373,000 associations, 78.1M residents, $124.2B assessments, ~$31B reserves); Association Reserves archive via RapidEye Sept 2026 compilation (>100,000 studies, 1986–2025): 74% under 70% funded, 34% under 30% funded.

## The join

Budget page (assessment income, reserve line, components, waiver language) → SIRS/study requirements for that state → does the reserve line match the study? For FL condos ≥3 stories: is there waiver language on a SIRS item that §718.112 now forbids? For any condo: reserve allocation as % of assessment income vs. 10% today and 15% for post-Jan-2027 applications.

## The verdict

- **Matches the study** — reserve line consistent with the study.
- **Does not match** — reserve line below the study or missing required components.
- **Page too thin** — can't tell from the uploaded page.
- **Waiver flag (FL)** — waiver language on a SIRS item no longer allowed for budgets adopted on/after Dec 31, 2024.
- **Lender-test line** — reserve % of assessment income next to the 10%/15% thresholds (informational, not a warrantability verdict).

## Guardrails

- Never accuse the board of breach — willful SIRS failure can be pleaded as fiduciary breach, but that pleading is a lawyer's work; the checker's work is the label.
- Never file a complaint (e.g., to the Florida Division) or a records request as a first action; never challenge a special assessment automatically. Models draft; the owner signs. Irreversible steps stay human.
- Never tell the owner the unit is unwarrantable — show the line the lender will divide.
- Charge only after the page proves a mismatch.

## Limitations

- The 70%-funded line is industry convention, not statute — useful benchmark, not law (stated in essay).
- Rules are state-specific (FL SIRS, CA 5551); a national checker needs a state tag on every page, and the essay starts with Florida only (stated in essay).
- The Fact Book's $124.2B pool says nothing about any single building (stated in essay).
- Governing documents are a swamp — the checker deliberately reads only the reserve line, so document-level nuance is out of scope by design (stated in essay).
- Special assessments from already-landed repair bills (e.g., CA balcony work) are a separate product surface (inferred).
